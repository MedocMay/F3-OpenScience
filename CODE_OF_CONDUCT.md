# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## A note on technical discussion

The core of this project is **deciding what can be trusted**. Technical disagreement is not
only normal but necessary — especially arguments about whether a given criterion overreaches. Please:

- **Address the criterion, not the person**: question the logic, not the proposer's competence
- **Argue with evidence**: one reachability regression case beats ten assertions
- **Admit uncertainty**: saying "I'm not sure this boundary is right" is not a weakness —
  it is exactly the quality this project demands of its code, and should demand of its discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to
**maymedoc@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a
response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to
maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.
